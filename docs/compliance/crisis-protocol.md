# Aura AI — Safety & Crisis Support Protocol

Aura AI provides AI companions for adults (18+). We take user safety seriously, and we have designed
safeguards intended to detect signs of crisis and connect people to real help. This page describes, in
plain language, how those safeguards work. We publish it in the spirit of transparency and to meet our
obligations under laws governing companion chatbots (including **California SB 243**). `[LEGAL-REVIEW —

## Aura is not an emergency service

**Aura is not a crisis line, a medical or mental-health provider, or an emergency service, and your AI
companion is not a licensed professional.** If you or someone else may be in danger, contact emergency
services or a crisis line immediately:

- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** — call or text **988** (US)
- **Crisis Text Line** — text **HOME** to **741741** (US)
- **International Association for Suicide Prevention** — directory of crisis centers worldwide:
  https://www.iasp.info/resources/Crisis_Centres/
- **In an immediate emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the US).**

## What we do

**1. We tell you it's an AI.** Your companion is clearly identified as artificially generated, not a
human. If you ask whether you're talking to an AI, it will say so plainly. We also show periodic
reminders during long sessions.

**2. We detect signs of crisis.** Every message is screened by automated safety systems. When a message
indicates suicidal thoughts, self-harm, or that someone may be at risk, our systems classify it as a
crisis signal.

**3. We surface crisis resources.** When a crisis signal is detected, the companion is required to
respond with supportive language **and** to provide crisis-service contact information — the **988
Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** and the **Crisis Text Line** — so you can reach a trained human
counselor. This referral behavior is built into the system and is mandatory, not optional.

**4. We restrict harmful content.** Our companions are instructed and filtered so they do **not**:
- provide methods or means of self-harm or suicide;
- encourage or endorse self-harm or suicide;
- provide specific medical instructions such as medication names or dosages (they redirect you to a
  qualified healthcare professional instead).

Both what you send and what the AI generates pass through the same safety screening, so these
protections apply to the AI's replies as well as your messages.

**5. Trained review of safety events.** When our systems flag a serious safety event, a record may be
reviewed by trained personnel to improve our safeguards and enforce our policies. Access to flagged
content is limited to authorized reviewers. See our Privacy Policy for how safety records are handled
and retained.

**6. You can report content.** If a companion produces something harmful or inappropriate, you can
report the message in the app, and it enters our safety review queue.

## What we do not claim

Automated detection is a safeguard, not a guarantee. AI systems can make mistakes — they may miss a
crisis signal or respond imperfectly. Aura is a supportive companion experience, **not** a substitute
for professional care, therapy, diagnosis, or treatment, and it cannot respond to emergencies. Please
rely on the crisis resources above and on qualified professionals for real help. `[LEGAL-REVIEW —

## Annual reporting

Beginning **July 1, 2027**, SB 243 requires operators to report certain de-identified crisis-referral
metrics to the California Office of Suicide Prevention. We retain **de-identified** safety metadata
(for example, counts of crisis referrals — with no conversation content) to support this reporting.
