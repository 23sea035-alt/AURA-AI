## 2. Description of the Service — The Companion Is AI

Aura AI is an iOS application that lets you have one-to-one text and voice conversations with AI-powered personas ("**companions**") that can remember facts about you across conversations to personalize the experience. The Service currently offers a set of vetted personas that you can customize along a limited set of predefined options.

**Your companion is artificial intelligence. It is not a human being, is not conscious, is not sentient, and is not a real person.** Every message and voice reply from a companion is generated automatically by software. A companion has no feelings, no independent existence, and no awareness, even when it expresses warmth, empathy, affection, or personality, and even if it says otherwise in conversation. Any impression that you are talking to a real person, a friend, a partner, or a licensed professional is a product of the AI's design and is **not real**.

You acknowledge and agree that:

- you are interacting with an automated conversational system, not a person;
- the companion's "memories," personality, and emotional expressions are simulated features of the software, not genuine relationships or mental states; and
- you should not rely on a companion as you would rely on a real person or a qualified professional.

We disclose the AI nature of the Service in onboarding, periodically within conversations, and whenever you ask a companion whether it is human. This disclosure is a core safety and transparency feature and may not be disabled.
